I want a simple math study app geared for students that have to do some low involvement activities or jobs on the side but want to be able to study effectively on the phone. For now the simplest basic idea is grant an apikey for openrouter (default to openai/gpt-6-luna), the students can ask to generate a lesson on some concept or on a whole group of concepts, even generate a whole course lesson by lesson, and the ai generates both an accompanying 'lecture notes' and a narration to be read by an on-device model like kokoro or another openrouter model. But we need to make the generated sound adjustable speed and proper cadence with proper pauses as if it was a lecture not just read normally without time to think it. The app should include the functionality to listen to these and offer a very intuitive player (double tap right to advance a few seconds, left to go back), visualize the subtitles of the lecture appropiate for small smartphone screens, read the accompanying notes also in a fit for small screens full latex symbol format (its for grad math lessons), and finally read a full pdf version of the notes inside the app. Also all well organized.
